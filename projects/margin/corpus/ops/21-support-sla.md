# Support and SLA

## Channels

| Channel | Hours | First response |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Discord (traders-help) | 24/7 community | minutes (community) |
| In-dashboard ticket | 24/5 (Sun 22:00 UTC – Fri 21:00 UTC) | < 4 hours |
| Email support@hydrogenprop.example | 24/7 | < 12 hours |
| Payout urgent line | payout windows only | < 1 hour |

## Scope

- The support team can: check breach logs, replay any disputed trade against
  the master tape, confirm EA approval status, fix whitelisted wallets.
- The support team CANNOT: override drawdown breaches (ever), waive the
  consistency rule, or accelerate payouts outside the window. Asking for a
  breach override gets a canned no — the breach log is immutable.

## Dispute process

1. Ticket with trade IDs within 24 hours of the disputed fill/breach.
2. Risk desk replays the tape; response within 2 business days.
3. If feed error is confirmed, trades are adjusted; if not, the replay result
   is final.

## Status page

Platform status and roll calendars live at status.hydrogenprop.example;
feed incidents are posted there within 5 minutes of detection.
