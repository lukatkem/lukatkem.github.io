# KYC and AML Policy

Identity verification is required before the first payout and before EA
registration.

## Required documents

1. Government photo ID (passport, national ID, driver's license).
2. Proof of address under 3 months old (utility bill, bank statement).
3. For payouts over $10,000 cumulative: source-of-funds declaration.

## One person, one identity

- One identity may hold up to 5 funded accounts.
- Family members may NOT share payment instruments — payout rails must match
  the verified trader.
- Business accounts (LLC payouts) are allowed with company registration docs
  and UBO disclosure.

## AML triggers

Manual review is triggered by: rapid pass patterns (pass in < 2 days twice in
a row), identical tick-level trade sequences across unrelated accounts,
payout requests to third parties, or sanctions-list hits.

## Sanctions

Residents of the following are not accepted: OFAC comprehensive list
countries, and jurisdictions where prop trading is locally prohibited. VPN
usage during KYC is grounds for permanent ban.

## Data handling

KYC documents are stored with the payment processor, not on trading servers.
Retention is 5 years post-account-closure per AML regulation.
