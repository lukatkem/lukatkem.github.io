# Payout Methods and Fees

## Available methods

| Method     | Min payout | Fee           | Arrival          |
|------------|------------|---------------|------------------|
| USDC (Base)| $100       | none          | same day         |
| Bank wire  | $500       | $25 per wire  | 1–3 business days|
| Rise       | $100       | 1% (max $15)  | same–1 day       |
| Wise       | $100       | none          | same–1 day       |

## Details

- **USDC on Base** is the default and fastest rail. The firm sends USDC to a
  whitelisted wallet address only; the address can be changed once per 30 days
  and requires 2FA + email confirm.
- **Bank wire** requires the legal name on the bank account to match KYC
  exactly. Mismatched names bounce and cost a $25 return fee.
- **Wise/Rise** are available in 80+ countries; sanctions-listed jurisdictions
  are excluded regardless of method.

## Tax

HydrogenProp does not withhold taxes. Traders are independent contractors and
receive a monthly statement; US traders get a 1099-NEC at year end when annual
payouts exceed $600.

## Failed payouts

- Wrong address: USDC to a non-whitelisted address is unrecoverable. The
  whitelisting waiting period (24h + email confirm) exists to prevent this.
- Currency conversion is done by the receiving platform, not the firm.
