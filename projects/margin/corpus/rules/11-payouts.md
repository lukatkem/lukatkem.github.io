# Payouts — Schedule, Splits, Requirements

## Cycle

- Payout window: **bi-weekly** (14 calendar days).
- Request window opens on the 14th and 28th of each month, stays open 72 hours.
- Processing takes 1–2 business days after the window closes.

## Requirements to request

1. Account is ≥ 30 days old (first payout), then bi-weekly.
2. Minimum profit since last payout: **$500** on $50k+ accounts, **$250** on
   smaller sizes.
3. Balance above the trailing drawdown + $100 buffer.
4. Consistency rule satisfied for the window (best day ≤ 40% of window profit).
5. Minimum 3 trading days in the window.
6. KYC completed.

## Split schedule

| Payouts made | Split (≤ $50k accounts) | Split ($100k+) |
|--------------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| 1–3          | 80%                      | 85%             |
| 4–6          | 85%                      | 90%             |
| 7+           | 90%                      | 90%             |

## Caps

- First payout is capped at 50% of window profit (anti-fraud ramp); from the
  second payout the full window profit is payable.
- Maximum payout per cycle: $25,000 on a $100k account. Larger amounts roll to
  the next cycle automatically.

## Refusal and clawback

- Payouts are refused (not clawed back) if a breach is detected in the window.
- Confirmed rule violations discovered within 30 days of a payout void future
  payouts equal to the amount lost to the violation; the paid amount itself is
  not reclaimed except in fraud cases.
