# Refund Policy

## Evaluation fee refund

The evaluation fee is refunded with the **first payout** if:

1. The account was passed without any hard-breach warning.
2. The refund is requested within 30 days of the first payout.
3. The trader has not opened a new evaluation on the same size in parallel.

Refund goes out on the same rail as the payout, 1–2 business days after the
payout settles.

## No-refund cases

- Failed evaluation (any drawdown breach, hard or soft).
- Expired evaluation where both free extensions were used and 90 days passed.
- Chargebacks filed instead of a support request — these permanently ban the
  identity.

## Free resets

- Every failed evaluation gets one **free reset** within 14 days of failure
  (same size, same rules). The reset restarts Phase 1.
- Second and later resets cost 50% of the evaluation fee.

## Unused phase discounts

Passing Phase 1 within 10 calendar days grants a 25% discount code for the
next evaluation of any size.
