# Consistency Rule — Best Day Cap

The consistency rule exists to prevent gambling one lucky day through the
evaluation. It applies to Phase 1, Phase 2, and payout processing.

## The rule

**No single trading day may account for more than 40% of total profit.**

## How it is checked

- Applies at the moment you request a pass or a payout — not continuously.
- Best day profit ÷ total profit must be ≤ 0.40.
- If exceeded, the account is not failed. The trader must keep trading (or the
  payout is delayed) until the ratio is satisfied.

## Worked examples

### Example 1 — passes

- Total profit: $8,000. Best day: $3,000.
- Ratio = 37.5% → inside the 40% cap. Pass.

### Example 2 — blocked

- Total profit: $8,000. Best day: $4,500.
- Ratio = 56% → blocked. Trader needs total profit ≥ $11,250 with the same best
  day (4,500 ÷ 0.40), or a smaller best-day figure.

### Example 3 — minimum trading days interplay

- Trader hits the 8% target in 3 days with one day at 70% of profit.
- The pass is withheld until the ratio is fixed. Trading more days with
  moderate results is the standard fix.

## Payout consistency

For funded payouts the same 40% best-day cap applies to the payout window's
profit. A funded trader whose best day is 55% of window profit gets the payout
deferred to the next bi-weekly window.

## Strategy guidance

- Aim for no more than ~30% of profit on any single day while in evaluation.
- Two to four green days of similar size is the cleanest pass pattern.
- Red days do not help the ratio; they shrink the denominator.
