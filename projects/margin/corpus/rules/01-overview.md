# HydrogenProp — Firm Overview

HydrogenProp is a proprietary trading firm that funds retail traders through a
two-phase evaluation. Traders never trade real firm capital until they pass both
phases and sign the funded trader agreement.

## Account sizes and fees

| Account size | Evaluation fee | Funded size | Profit split |
|--------------|----------------|-------------|--------------|
| $10,000      | $59            | $10,000     | 80%          |
| $25,000      | $129           | $25,000     | 80%          |
| $50,000      | $229           | $50,000     | 80–85%       |
| $100,000     | $429           | $100,000    | 85–90%       |
| $200,000     | $829           | $200,000    | 90%          |

## Key facts

- Instruments: futures (CME micros and minis), forex majors, and select CFDs.
- Leverage on futures accounts is exchange margin; forex accounts get 1:30 retail.
- The evaluation fee is refundable with the first payout (see refunds policy).
- Accounts are simulated environments that mirror real market data feeds.
- One trader may hold a maximum of 5 funded accounts at once.
- Minimum trader age is 18 and KYC is required before any payout.

## Profit split progression

- Standard split is 80% trader / 20% firm on the first payout cycle.
- After 3 consecutive profitable payouts the split rises to 85%.
- After 6 consecutive profitable payouts on a $100k or larger account the split
  rises to 90%.

## What the firm does NOT do

- HydrogenProp does not lend money, charge interest, or manage client funds.
- Funded accounts are demo accounts on aggregated liquidity; "capital" refers to
  the firm's risk allocation, not a wire transfer to the trader.
