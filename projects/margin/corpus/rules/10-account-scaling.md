# Account Scaling Plan

Funded accounts grow on a performance-based schedule.

## Scaling schedule

| Condition | Reward |
|-----------|--------|
| 10% net profit since start (or last scale) | +25% account size |
| 2 consecutive profitable payout cycles before scale request | required |
| No hard breach warnings in last 30 days | required |
| Scale frequency | at most once every 14 days |

## Example ladder ($50,000 start)

- Start: $50,000 funded.
- +$5,000 profit → account becomes $62,500.
- Next +$6,250 (10% of 62.5k) → $78,125.
- Third scale reaches $97,656, crossing the $100k ceiling where the 90% split
  becomes available.

## Rules

- Scaling increases the firm's risk allocation; the extra size is NOT paid out
  to the trader until the scaled account produces profit.
- Drawdown on a scaled account recalculates: 10% trailing from the new higher
  base (with the standard +6% cap mechanism).
- Scaling requests are made in the dashboard and processed within 2 business
  days. Trading continues during processing.
- Missing the 14-day minimum resets nothing — you just request later.

## Downscaling

Two consecutive losing cycles (cycle loss > 5%) cut the account back one step
(25%). The trader keeps the profit already paid out; the drawdown re-anchors to
the reduced base.
