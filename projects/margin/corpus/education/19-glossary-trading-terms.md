# Glossary — Trading Terms

## Risk terms

- **Drawdown** — decline from a peak equity/balance value to the current value.
- **Max daily loss** — the firm's 5% equity-based intraday limit; resets 00:00 UTC.
- **Trailing drawdown** — drawdown level that follows the highest end-of-day
  balance (funded accounts, post-payout).
- **High-water mark** — the highest end-of-day balance achieved on the account.
- **R multiple** — profit/loss expressed in units of initial risk (1R = the
  dollar amount risked at entry).

## Order types

- **Limit** — fill at price or better. No negative slippage.
- **Market** — immediate fill at best available; slippage possible.
- **Stop** — becomes a market order when triggered; gap risk applies.
- **Breach** — crossing a firm limit (daily loss, max drawdown, consistency,
  news window) as defined in the rules docs.

## Costs

- **Commission** — per-side or round-turn fee per lot/contract (see execution doc).
- **Spread** — difference between bid and ask; the de facto cost of market orders.
- **Swap** — overnight interest on CFD/forex positions (see overnight fees).
- **Slippage** — difference between expected and actual fill on market orders.

## Timing

- **RTH** — regular trading hours (US futures: 09:30–16:00 ET).
- **Rollover window** — 21:55–23:05 UTC daily, liquidity thins, spreads widen.
- **First notice date** — futures deadline after which expiring contracts are
  force-closed on the firm (see weekend/holding doc).

## Firm terms

- **Evaluation** — the paid two-phase challenge (8% then 5% targets).
- **Funded account** — simulated account with the firm's risk allocation after
  passing; profit split 80–90%.
- **Payout cycle** — the bi-weekly window used for payout requests.
- **Hard breach** — instant-fail violation (drawdown, prohibited practices).
- **Soft breach** — warning-level violation (news window profit removal).
