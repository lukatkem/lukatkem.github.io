On a bright Saturday, Ivy wandered to the corner bakery with a sandwich and a book in a canvas bag. An old man at the corner bakery handed Theo a lost puppy and said it had been waiting a long time. At home, Ivy wiped off the dirt, tightened the loose parts, and painted a tiny blue star on it. On Sunday everyone gathered at the river bank to see it. There was cake, laughter, and a round of applause.
