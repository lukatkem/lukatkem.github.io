On a bright Saturday, Ada wandered to the school garden with a sandwich and a book in a canvas bag. While cutting across the grass, Sam spotted a tiny lantern glinting between two paving stones. It took three evenings of patient work to clean and repair, but every minute felt worth it. The story spread through the neighborhood, and for months people called it Sam's little miracle.
