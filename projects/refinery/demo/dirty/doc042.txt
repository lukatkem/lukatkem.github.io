Theo had one job that afternoon: deliver a package to the rooftop before the sun went down. That was where Max found a small kite, tucked behind a bench as if someone had left it in a hurry. At home, Leo wiped off the dirt, tightened the loose parts, and painted a tiny blue star on it. On Sunday everyone gathered at the quiet beach to see it. There was cake, laughter, and a round of applause.
