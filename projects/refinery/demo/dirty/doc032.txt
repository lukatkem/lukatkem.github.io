Theo woke up early and walked to the corner bakery. The morning was cool and the streets were quiet. That was where Mila found a red mitten, tucked behind a bench as if someone had left it in a hurry. At home, Sam wiped off the dirt, tightened the loose parts, and painted a tiny blue star on it. A week later Mila returned it to the very spot, for the next curious person to find and love.
