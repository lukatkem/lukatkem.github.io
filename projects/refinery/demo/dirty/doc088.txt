On a bright Saturday, Ivy wandered to the river bank with a sandwich and a book in a canvas bag. Ivy almost stepped on a small kite, stopped, knelt down, and picked it up with both hands. It took three evenings of patient work to clean and repair, but every minute felt worth it. A week later Max returned it to the very spot, for the next curious person to find and love.
