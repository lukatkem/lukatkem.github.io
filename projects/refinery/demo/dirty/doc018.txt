On a bright Saturday, Nia wandered to the rooftop with a sandwich and a book in a canvas bag. That was where Theo found a red mitten, tucked behind a bench as if someone had left it in a hurry. At home, Max wiped off the dirt, tightened the loose parts, and painted a tiny blue star on it. On Sunday everyone gathered at the quiet beach to see it. There was cake, laughter, and a round of applause.
