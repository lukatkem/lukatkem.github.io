Mila had one job that afternoon: deliver a package to the river bank before the sun went down. An old man at the corner bakery handed Theo a lost puppy and said it had been waiting a long time. At home, Sam wiped off the dirt, tightened the loose parts, and painted a tiny blue star on it. On Sunday everyone gathered at the pine forest to see it. There was cake, laughter, and a round of applause.
