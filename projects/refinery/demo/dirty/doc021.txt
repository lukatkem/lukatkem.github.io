On a bright Saturday, Nia wandered to the quiet beach with a sandwich and a book in a canvas bag. An old man at the corner bakery handed Theo a warm scarf and said it had been waiting a long time. At home, Nia wiped off the dirt, tightened the loose parts, and painted a tiny blue star on it. A week later Ivy returned it to the very spot, for the next curious person to find and love.
