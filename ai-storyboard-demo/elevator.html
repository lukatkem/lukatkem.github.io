THE LAST ELEVATOR

A vertical-drama cold open in 9 frames. On the night of her engagement party, Mia learns who her fiancé really is — from a message preview in an elevator mirror. One location, two faces, one phone: the cheapest scene to keep consistent across AI generations, and the hardest to fake continuity in. That trade-off is the point.

02 · Dramatic Beats

Beat Breakdown

Each beat is a generation unit: one emotional state per beat keeps faces, wardrobe and lighting coherent when images are produced in batches.

00:00 – 00:22 · Beat 1

The Ascent

Flirty banter, champagne, celebration. A phone buzzes and is silenced — read as party logistics. Plant: Mia sees everything in the mirror before we ever cut wide on it.

00:22 – 00:48 · Beat 2

The Message

The reveal lands in three images: mirror watch → eyes hold → macro on the screen. No dialogue. The smile stays; the eyes leave.

00:48 – 01:05 · Beat 3

The Smile

Doors open, applause, gold light. Mia takes control of the frame — collar fix, "Not here." — and exits first. Tag on the abandoned coupe: she's already three moves ahead.

05 · AI Image Prompts

ChatGPT-Ready Prompts

Every prompt is assembled from four locked blocks — style, two character locks, location — then the shot. All nine frames were generated from these; paste-ready.

MIA — CHARACTER LOCK

Late 20s, shoulder-length dark brown hair tucked behind her left ear, small mole below her left eye, red satin slip dress, thin gold hoop earrings.

DANIEL — CHARACTER LOCK

Early 30s, short black hair, clean-shaven, strong jaw, charcoal suit, white shirt with open collar, black leather watch.

LOCATION LOCK

Luxury apartment elevator: black marble, gold trim, full-height mirror on the back wall, brass rail. Beyond the doors: cream roses, warm gold string lights.

STYLE LOCK

Cinematic photorealism, vertical 9:16, shallow DOF, 35mm, soft contrast, muted palette with a deep red accent, fine film grain.

CONTINUITY DISCIPLINE

One Gemini thread for all nine frames — Mia's mole + tucked hair + red satin and Daniel's watch + open collar locked in words and re-checked on every gen; the mirror, brass rail and gold doors kept as fixed geography; retakes logged per shot (S4 key light moved to kill a double reflection in the hoops).

WHAT THIS SHOWS

A third format (vertical drama vs 16:9 film and graphic-novel action), a two-hander acting scene with no dialogue to hide behind, and the hardest AI constraint set: two recurring faces in one mirrored room across nine generations.
