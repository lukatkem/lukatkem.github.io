01 · The Story

Three Acts, One Run

The sequence is cut into three acts, each a generation block with its own location and emotional state — the same unit structure used to keep AI frames coherent in batches.

P1 – P18 · Act 1

The Tracking

Rain-soaked neon alleys. A lone detective follows a glowing magenta artifact through the city — standoff, chase, and the first contact: the artifact thieves have a name for her already.

P19 – P27 · Act 2

The Infiltration

Sarah's AR interface locks the target. Through a night market crowd to the vault door: biometric scans, device sync, the complex lock identified — Sarah primes the final transfer.

P28 – P36 · Act 3

The Core

Inside the server vault: the biometric key turns, the green data core wakes, "ACCESS GRANTED… 00:03" — and the duo walks out toward a new path with the packet.

Locked Prompt Set

Same discipline as Same Bread: four locked blocks — style, two character locks, location — then the shot. Every panel generated from these; paste-ready.

SARAH — CHARACTER LOCK

Woman detective, mid-30s, black bob with straight bangs, clear glasses, long camel trench coat over dark turtleneck, black gloves, calm focused expression, carries a slim tablet device.

SPIKE — CHARACTER LOCK

Late 20s man, short platinum-blond undercut, dark leather-synth coat over grey hoodie, black fingerless gloves, cocky half-smile, carries a slate tablet, lean build.

LOCATION LOCK

Rain-soaked neon cyberpunk alleys (magenta/cyan signs), a crowded night market, and a high-security server vault: towering black racks with green status lights, glass biometric lock panel, central glowing green data sphere, cold volumetric haze.

CONTINUITY DISCIPLINE

Gemini threads with Sarah's bob+glasses+camel coat and Spike's platinum undercut locked in words and re-checked on every gen; the artifact's magenta glow, the rain and the rack corridor kept as fixed geography; SFX lettering specified per panel so the model never invents its own. 36 panels across 4 locations with zero character drift.

WHAT THIS SHOWS

A different genre (cyberpunk action), a different visual style (graphic-novel vs photoreal), a full three-act story arc instead of a single scene — same locked-prompt workflow at 3× the scale. The loop transfers to whatever script your production brings.
