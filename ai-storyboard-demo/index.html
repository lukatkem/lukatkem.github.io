01 · The Scene

Original Screenplay

Cold open for episode 1 of a vertical drama. One location, two faces, one phone — the cheapest scene to keep consistent across AI generations, and the hardest to fake continuity in. That trade-off is the point.

On the night of her engagement party, Mia learns who her fiancé really is — from a message preview in an elevator mirror.

INT. LUXURY APARTMENT ELEVATOR — NIGHT S1 The doors close on MIA (late 20s, red satin slip dress) and DANIEL (early 30s, charcoal suit). He hands her a champagne coupe. elevator hums upward S2 In his other hand, his PHONE buzzes — face-up. S3 Daniel flips it toward himself. Too late. In the MIRROR, Mia's reflection is already reading the preview. S4 Close on Mia. Her smile doesn't move. Her eyes do. MESSAGE PREVIEW on Daniel's phone screen V: You told her I was your assistant. I'm at the party. S6 Daniel reads it. The jaw goes tight. S7 DING. The doors open on warm light and applause. Both faces catch gold. S8 Mia steps out FIRST. Fixes Daniel's collar. MIA perfect smile, low Not here. S9 The doors close on the empty elevator — and her champagne coupe, left on the rail.
02 · Dramatic Beats

Beat Breakdown

Each beat is a generation unit: one emotional state per beat keeps faces, wardrobe and lighting coherent when images are produced in batches.

00:00 – 00:22

The Ascent

Flirty banter, champagne, celebration. A phone buzzes and is silenced — read as party logistics. Plant: Mia sees everything in the mirror before we ever cut wide on it.

00:22 – 00:48

The Message

The reveal lands in three images: mirror watch → eyes hold → macro on the screen. No dialogue. The smile stays; the eyes leave.

00:48 – 01:05

The Smile

Doors open, applause, gold light. Mia takes control of the frame — collar fix, "Not here." — and exits first. Tag on the abandoned coupe: she's already three moves ahead.

06 · AI Image Prompts

ChatGPT-Ready Prompts

Every prompt is assembled from four locked blocks — style, character, location, then the shot. Paste as-is; no retouching needed. Changelog discipline below.

MIA — CHARACTER LOCK

Late 20s, shoulder-length dark brown hair tucked behind her left ear, small mole below her left eye, red satin slip dress, thin gold hoop earrings.

DANIEL — CHARACTER LOCK

Early 30s, short black hair, clean-shaven, strong jaw, charcoal suit, white shirt with open collar, black leather watch.

LOCATION LOCK

Luxury apartment elevator: black marble, gold trim, full-height mirror on the back wall, brass rail. Beyond the doors: cream roses, warm gold string lights.

STYLE LOCK

Cinematic photorealism, vertical 9:16, shallow DOF, 35mm, soft contrast, muted palette with a deep red accent, fine film grain.

PROMPT CHANGELOG — SAMPLE

S4 v3 → v4: key light moved from camera-left to top-center to kill the double reflection in the hoops; framing raised to keep the neckline in frame. Logged per shot — same record kept for every retake.

RETAKES & SEEDS

One seed per character, edits in place instead of fresh rolls; S4 flagged as the most-iterated frame so its history is the deepest. Failed gens never re-rolled blind — the prompt moves first.
