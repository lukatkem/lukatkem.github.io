Hydro-1 · two brains, zero servers

Before anything loads, the terms:

  • The tiny brain is the point. Hydro-1 is a ~10.7M-parameter GPT trained from scratch on a laptop. It babbles charmingly — that's what from-scratch looks like at this scale, and it's the lesson.
  • Real answers are optional and are yours. Add your own free API key (Google AI Studio or Groq — 30-second signups) and the page calls that provider directly from your browser. The key is saved in your browser's local storage only and is sent nowhere except the provider you chose.
  • This site has no server and collects nothing. No cookies, no analytics, no backend. Your messages exist on your device.
  • Don't type personal information. It's a demo — build the habit.
