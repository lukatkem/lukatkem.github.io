Hydro-1 · in your browser

A language model trained from scratch — no pretrained weights, no APIs. Before it loads, the terms:

  • It is tiny, on purpose. ~10.7M parameters (ChatGPT-class models: hundreds of billions). Expect charming nonsense, spelling slips and wandering stories — not facts.
  • Everything runs on your machine. The ~21 MB of weights download once and the transformer computes locally. Your messages never leave your device — there is no server to send them to.
  • Don't type personal information. It's a demo, and it wouldn't go anywhere — but build the habit.
  • Output may be wrong or odd. The model is educational — it shows what a from-scratch GPT honestly looks like at this scale.
